Sales Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data to uncover key business insights related to revenue, customer behavior, payment methods, and product performance.
The dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of sales performance across different dimensions such as time, branches, categories, and payment methods.

---

Objectives
- Analyze total sales performance
- Understand customer distribution
- Identify the most used payment methods
- Track monthly sales trends
- Compare branch performance
- Evaluate category and brand contributions

---

Tools & Technologies
- Excel  (Dashboard & Visualization)
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Analysis Techniques

---

Key Insights

Total Performance
- Total Sales: **$974,693**
- Total Customers: **638**

---

Sales by Payment Method
- PayPal generated the highest revenue
- Credit Card comes second
- Cash and Bank Transfer follow

---

Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales started strong in January (~$97K)
- Slight fluctuations throughout the year
- Lowest point observed around November (~$74K)

---

Sales by Branches
- Branch 3 achieved the highest sales
- Branch 4 and Branch 1 show strong performance
- Branch 5 has the lowest sales

---

Sales by Categories & Brands
- Fashion category leads overall sales
- Electronics and Home follow
- Brand contribution varies across categories
